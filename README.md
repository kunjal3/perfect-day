# PERFECT DAY

### 24 hours. 24 people. 24 different versions of a perfect day.

We all live through the same 24 hours, but we don't experience them the same way.

For one person, 12:00 AM might be the perfect time to finally fall asleep.  
For someone else, it's when their night is just beginning.

At 8:00 AM, one person might be rushing to get their children ready for school, while another might still be asleep.

**Perfect Day** is an interactive journey through 24 hours, where each hour represents a different person, their situation, and what makes that particular moment perfect for them.

Explore the clock and discover the contrast between different lives, routines and priorities.

Because what feels ordinary to one person can mean everything to another.

> **Same 24 hours. Different lives. Different definitions of perfect.**

---

## ✦ THE EXPERIENCE

- 🕐 Explore all 24 hours through an interactive clock
- 👤 Meet a different person at every hour
- 🌍 Discover different lifestyles, situations and priorities
- ↔️ Navigate between hours
- ✦ Use **Surprise Me** to discover a random perspective
- 📖 Explore selected stories through the timeline

---

## ✦ THE IDEA

There is no single definition of a **Perfect Day**.

Someone might be starting their day while someone else is ending theirs.  
Someone might be working while another person is spending time with family.  
Someone might be chasing a goal while someone else simply wants to rest.

**Perfect Day** is a reminder that everyone has a different life — and therefore, a different idea of what makes a day perfect.
